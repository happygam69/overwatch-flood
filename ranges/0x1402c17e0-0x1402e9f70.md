## The structural story of this pass

**1. Block size collapses from 3-digit to 2-digit, and records move into the blocks.**
Block K (started in `0123`) turns out to be the largest block yet — **209 regs** (7 in `0123`
+ 150 + 52 here, run counts from the script). Then, instead of another 100–200-reg block, the
sea alternates with **1-, 2-, 3-, 4-, 5-, 6-, 8-, 11-, 23-, 32-, 33-reg groups**, and nearly
every group is immediately followed by its `{id,hash}` row(s). Pattern, measured:
`reg ×N → record ×1..3 → reg ×M → …`. Examples: `FUN_1402d8c70` (single reg) → `FUN_1402d8d80`
